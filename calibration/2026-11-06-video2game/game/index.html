Dockstack

Load the boat before it sails.

How to play

  1. Tap, click or press Space to drop the crate from the crane.
  2. A crate stays only if its middle lands over the crate or deck below.
  3. The hook speeds up and the boat rocks, so time each drop.
  4. Load all 15 crates. Most crates wins, then the tallest stack.

A new shipment every day, the same for everyone.
Press M to mute the sound.
